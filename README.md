# My Favorite Movie: Rio

## Why I Love It
**Rio** is a fun, colorful animated movie about a rare blue macaw named Blu who travels to *Rio de Janeiro*. I love the music, the humor, and the amazing scenery.

## Main Characters
- Blu
- Jewel
- Nico
- Pedro
- Rafael
- Nigel (the villain)

## My Favorite Things About It
1. The soundtrack and the carnival scenes
2. Blu and Jewel's adventure together
3. The funny side characters

> "Blu is a bird who can't fly... yet!"
